# M3 — Base & economy (first part)

Code written ahead (2026-11-23, branch `feat/night-groundwork`). Nothing to
build in the editor: everything is created at play. This part covers selling,
money, reputation, the shop and the field guides. Still to come in M3: the
identification keys of the books and the test kits in examine mode, the
pharmacist check, the storage chest and the encyclopedia desk.

## What the code provides

- **Buyer's counter**: a board left of the door of the forester's house
  (*Mushroom buyer - field guides*). Look at it and press **E**: the counter
  opens with the mouse cursor; **E** again to leave.
  - Left: the basket as the buyer values it, a **Sell** button and **Throw away
    the refused ones**.
  - Right: the shop (field guides, test kits) with **Buy** buttons.
- **`MarketModel`** (pure, D-036): price = price per kg of the species × weight
  × quality × demand of the day × reputation.
  - *Quality* = age at picking (young and mature 100 %, old down to 30 %) ×
    freshness (halves every 3 biological days = 0.75 game day) × 0.5 if
    wormy (fixed by the specimen's seed, more often in old ones).
  - *Demand*: 1.5 kg per species per day at full price, then less
    (1 / (1 + sold / 1.5 kg)): variety pays.
  - *Reputation* 0–100 (start 50): ×0.7 to ×1.3; +2 per kg of good
    mushrooms sold.
  - Refused: species you cannot name (not *Identified*), rotten, inedible
    (−3 reputation each).
  - **A toxic mushroom in the basket** (even unknown to you): the buyer
    confiscates it and refuses the whole basket, fine 30 € and −20
    reputation, ban 1 game day; **deadly**: fine 250 €, −45, ban 3 days
    (D-013 option B). Throw away what you cannot name before selling.
- **Shop** (`Data/Economy/Shop.json`): 5 field guides (Boletes 40 €,
  Chanterelles 30 €, Amanitas 60 €, Brittlegills 50 €, Tricholomas and spring
  mushrooms 35 €) and 2 test kits (spore prints 2 €, reagents 6 €; used by the
  identification keys later). A guide is a knowledge source of kind `Book`
  (`Data/Knowledge/Sources.json`): bought, its species examined before become
  *Identified* at once.
- **Start**: 20 €. Money, reputation, ban, owned items are saved (M4).
- **Debug**: `sh.Economy.Status` (money, items, the buyer's offer per
  specimen), `sh.Economy.AddMoney 100`, `sh.Economy.Sell`,
  `sh.Economy.Buy BookBoletes`, `sh.Economy.Reset`.

## Test checklist

Start a game (the save of M4 may restore an older state: `sh.Save.Delete`,
then Stop and Play, for a new game).

- [ ] The board hangs left of the door, readable title; looking at it shows
      *The buyer's counter [E] Sell and buy*; E opens the counter, mouse
      visible, the player does not move or turn; E closes it.
- [ ] Pick a few chanterelles and fly agarics (`sh.Spawn.Goto
      CantharellusCibarius`), read the poster, examine them: the counter lists
      the chanterelles with a price and a quality, the fly agaric in red
      (*TOXIC*).
- [ ] **Throw away the refused ones**, then **Sell**: money goes up, the
      basket empties, *Sold n mushroom(s)…* in green.
- [ ] Sell again with a fly agaric in the basket: everything refused, fine,
      reputation down, the fly agaric is gone, the header shows the ban; the
      next day the buyer deals again.
- [ ] A mushroom kept 2 game days is refused as not fresh; selling 3 kg of
      chanterelles the same day pays less per kg at the end.
- [ ] Buy *Field guide: Boletes* (with `sh.Economy.AddMoney 50` if needed):
      the cep and the bitter bolete examined before are identified at once;
      the encyclopedia lists them; the button shows *Owned*.
- [ ] Automation: `ShroomHunt.Economy.*` green.

## UE concepts learned
- **UMG buttons built in C++** (`UButton::OnClicked` is a dynamic delegate:
  one small `UObject` handler per button carries the item id).
- **Input modes**: `FInputModeGameAndUI` + mouse cursor while a screen is open,
  `FInputModeGameOnly` back.
- **Subsystem dependencies** (`InitializeDependency`): the economy needs the
  calendar, the species, the knowledge and the basket first.
